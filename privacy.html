Daily Tower Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

In short: the game keeps only what the leaderboards need: the player name you pick, your times, and a random number that tells the server which times are yours. There are no ads, no tracking, and no email or login. You can delete everything from inside the game at any time.

Who runs the game

Daily Tower is made by Stefan Tanasković, an independent developer in Serbia, who is responsible for the data described here. Contact: stefan.tanaskovic18@gmail.com.

What is sent to the server

  • A random player ID. The first time the game goes online, it signs in anonymously and gets a random ID. It is not linked to your name, phone number, email or any other account.
  • Your player name. You pick it on the first start. It is shown to everyone on the leaderboards.
  • Your times. For each day's tower: your best time, how many times you fell on that climb, and when you set it.

Like every website and app, the servers also see your device's internet (IP) address while the game is connected. It is used only to make the connection work and to keep the service safe, and it only stays in short-term server logs.

What stays on your device

Your settings (sound, music, chosen creature), today's best time and tries, your streak and your best times on past towers are saved on your device only. On the web they are kept in your browser's storage for this site. No cookies are used for tracking or advertising.

Why

This data is used only to run the daily leaderboards: to show your name and time, work out your place, and stop impossible times. It is never sold, never used for advertising and never shared with anyone else, apart from the service providers below that run the game.

Service providers

  • Supabase stores the player IDs, names and times, on servers in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany).
  • The company that hosts the game's website (or the app store you downloaded it from) delivers the game to your device.
  • If you choose to support the game with a tip, the payment is handled by that payment service (for example Ko-fi, PayPal, Google Play or the App Store) under its own privacy policy. The game never sees your payment details.

How long it is kept

Your name and times stay on the leaderboards, including the results of past days, until you delete them. If you clear your browser's storage or move to a new phone, the game starts over as a new player; your old name and times stay on the leaderboards until you ask for them to be deleted (see below).

Deleting your data

In the game, open Settings → Privacy → Delete my data. This removes your name, all your times on every leaderboard and your anonymous sign-in from the server straight away, and the game on your device starts over as a new player.

If you can no longer reach the game on the old device, email stefan.tanaskovic18@gmail.com with your player name and we will delete it.

Your rights

You can ask what data is kept about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, and object to how it is used. Email stefan.tanaskovic18@gmail.com. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority: in Serbia, the Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection, or the authority in your own country.

Children

The game is for everyone and does not ask for age, contact details or any personal information beyond a made-up player name. Please don't use your real name as your player name. Parents can delete a child's data with Delete my data or by email.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
